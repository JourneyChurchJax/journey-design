Use Footer to close any Journey page. It is the deepest field on the page.

```jsx
<Footer
  blurb={{title:'Journey Church', text:'Sundays 9:00 & 11:00 AM in Jacksonville.'}}
  columns={[{title:'Visit', links:[{label:'Plan a visit', href:'/visit/'}]}]}
  base={{left:'6225 Lake Gray Blvd Ste 2, Jacksonville, FL 32244', right:'© 2026 Journey Church'}}
/>
```

- Column heads are 0.72rem uppercase in #9a948a. Links are cream, hover red.
- Grain sits over the ink. No gradient, no divider other than the one hairline above the base row.
