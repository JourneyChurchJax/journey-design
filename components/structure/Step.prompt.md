Use Step for "how this works" sequences. Lay three or four across a `.grid3` or `.grid4`.

```jsx
<div className="grid3">
  <Step number="01" title="Park anywhere">Someone in a red shirt will point you at a door.</Step>
  <Step number="02" title="Check kids in">The desk is the first thing inside.</Step>
</div>
```

- Numerals are Inter Tight 600 in red, around 2.75rem.
- No circles, no badges, no connector lines. The numeral is the device.
