Use Meter to show campaign progress. Show progress or do not post the graphic.

```jsx
<Meter pct={66} left="Raised · $660,000" right="Goal · figure TBC" />
```

- 0.5rem tall, fully rounded, red fill on a 10% ink track.
- Both figures must come from a live source. A wrong dollar amount is worse than no graphic.
- On dark grounds the track flips to 14% cream automatically.
