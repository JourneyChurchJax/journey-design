Use Facts for service times, addresses, and event logistics. It is the one thing set identically every time.

```jsx
<Facts items={[
  {label:'When', value:'Sundays 9:00 & 11:00 AM'},
  {label:'Where', value:'6225 Lake Gray Blvd Ste 2'},
]} />
```

- Labels 0.72rem uppercase tracked 0.18em. Values Inter Tight 600.
- Dates read `Sun Oct 12 · 6 PM`. Times use the ampersand: `9:00 & 11:00 AM`.
- Confirm every date, room, and figure against a live system, never a document.
