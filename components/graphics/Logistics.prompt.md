Use Logistics on every event graphic. It is set identically every time.

```jsx
<Logistics items={[{label:'When', value:'Sun Oct 12 · 6 PM'},{label:'Where', value:'The Chapel'}]} />
```

- Label Inter 600, 22px, tracked 0.18em, uppercase, muted. Value Inter Tight 600, 36px.
- Dates as `Sun Oct 12 · 6 PM`. Times with the ampersand: `9:00 & 11:00 AM`.
- It sits directly above the logo so the eye finishes on when and where.
- No clock or map-pin icon. The label says "When".
