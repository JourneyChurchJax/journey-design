Use ArtText for the eyebrow-plus-headline pair on any artboard.

```jsx
<ArtText eyebrow="Healing Room · Quarterly" size={108} accent="prayed">Come and be</ArtText>
```

- Type floors on a 1080 square: eyebrow 26px, headline 86px, body 34px. Nothing smaller, ever.
- `series` borrows Archivo Expanded Black. Only big events and series art may.
- The eyebrow names the moment, the headline says it in a human sentence, and there are no exclamation points.
