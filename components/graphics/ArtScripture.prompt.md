Use ArtScripture for the Scripture card and the Scripture stage slide.

```jsx
<ArtScripture reference="Psalm 27:4" size={68}>
  One thing I have asked of the Lord … to <em>inquire in his temple</em>.
</ArtScripture>
```

- Newsreader never changes, even when a series brings its own typeface. That is the one thing a series never restyles.
- On a 1920 stage screen the floor is 80px.
- Pair with the mark alone, not the full lockup, when the card is Scripture only.
