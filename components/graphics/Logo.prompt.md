Use Logo anywhere the mark appears. Never retype the words "Journey Church" when you have the file.

```jsx
<Logo variant="horizontal" on="dark" width={360} corner="bottom-right" />
```

- The contract on a graphic: bottom-right at 360px on 1920-wide art, bottom-left at 280px on 1080 squares. Same corner, same size, every series, no exceptions.
- Clear space is the height of the J-circle on all four sides.
- Minimums: horizontal 120px on screen and 1.25in in print; mark 24px / 0.25in.
- Never the red mark on a photograph — use `on="photo"`, which reverses the whole lockup.
- Never stretched, rotated, outlined, shadowed, recolored, cropped, ghosted, or used as texture.
- Only PNGs exist. Anything printed larger than a flier is waiting on a vector file.
