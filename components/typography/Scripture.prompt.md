Use Scripture for any quoted verse or pull-quote. It is the only place the serif appears.

```jsx
<Scripture reference="Ephesians 2:22">
  In him you also are being built together into a dwelling place for God by the <em>Spirit</em>.
</Scripture>
```

- Never for headlines, UI copy, or the church's own voice.
- Never invent a reference. Confirm it against a real text.
- Scripture stays in Newsreader even inside bespoke series art. That rule never flexes.
