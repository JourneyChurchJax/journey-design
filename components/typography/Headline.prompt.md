Use Headline for any Journey headline — the church speaking in Inter Tight 800, with exactly one italic red accent word.

```jsx
<Headline as="h1" size="hero" accent="are">Come as you</Headline>
```

- `size`: `hero` (9.25rem cap, page openers) · `display` (4.75rem, section openers) · `h2` · `h3`.
- The accent word turns white at 95% opacity automatically inside `.on-dark`, `.on-deep`, and `.on-ember`.
- One accent per headline. If the beat word is "the" or "and", the headline is wrong, not the rule.
- Never use Newsreader here. Scripture is a different component.
