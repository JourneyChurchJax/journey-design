Use Eyebrow for the small uppercase label that names the moment above a headline.

```jsx
<Eyebrow dot>Journey Command · Brand</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow>Sundays 9:00 &amp; 11:00 AM</Eyebrow>
```

- 0.78rem, 0.22em tracking, weight 600, uppercase. On an artboard the floor is 26px.
- `dot` for hero and footer heads. `line` for the animated hairline.
- Red text under about 26px on cream must be #9a1818 — `tone="ember"` (the default) handles that.
