Happy Thanksgiving from Laura and me, and from our whole Journey Church family!

This basket comes from people who have been praying for you. We hope it fills your table this week. But we want it to say something too. You are loved. You are seen. You are not forgotten! Not by us, and not by God.

Maybe this year has been good to you. Maybe it's been the hardest one you've walked through. Maybe you're just tired. Here's what I know: Jesus hasn't gone anywhere. He is good, He is faithful, and He is closer than you think.

Oh, give thanks to the LORD, for He is good! For His mercy endures forever.Psalm 107:1 (NKJV)

It doesn't say His mercy lasts until you mess up. It says forever.

We'd love to meet you. You don't have to clean anything up or have it all figured out first. Come as you are! Maybe church has been home for years, or maybe it's been a long time. Either way, there's a place for you at Journey. Your kids get their own space too, from babies all the way through fifth grade.
