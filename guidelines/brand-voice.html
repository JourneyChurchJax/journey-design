How we write

  • Warm, clear, honest. Short and human over clever.
  • To one person considering a visit, not to an audience.
  • Say Jesus.
  • Keep the concrete logistics.
  • Service times use an ampersand: Sundays 9:00 & 11:00 AM.

Words we don't use

  • Blessed, anointed, season of, powerful, breakthrough, intercede.
  • "Encounter" as filler, "pursue God's presence," "stand in unity."
  • The test: if it could be lifted from any church email in the country, rewrite it.
