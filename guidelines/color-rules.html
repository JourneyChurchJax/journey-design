• One red thing per view. If two things are red, one is probably wrong.
  • Red is never a background — only a button, a pull-quote, a thin rule, or the mark.
  • Red text under about 26px on cream must be #9a1818. #ff3a3a fails contrast there.
  • There is no second color. No success green, no warning amber, no ministry palettes.
  • On dark and on photos the accent word turns white; red shows in the eyebrow or the mark, not both.
