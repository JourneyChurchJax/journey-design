iPhone setup and edit

Shoot dark to protect the stage lights; the preset brings it back.

Before you shoot

  • Native Camera app
  • Formats: ProRAW & Resolution Control on, ProRAW Max 48 MP
  • Clear 25 to 33 GB first (about 75 MB a shot)
  • Preserve Settings: all on except Action Mode
  • Grid and Level on · Live Photos off
  • RAW MAX badge on · exposure -1.0
  • Optical lenses only: 0.5x, 1x, 5x. Never pinch to zoom

Edit in Lightroom

  1. Preset first: Journey 2026. A preset applied later overwrites your edits.
  2. Fix: crop and straighten, white balance (real skin, not stage orange), exposure by eye, highlights down or shadows up as needed.
  3. Finish: vignette, sharpening and lens corrections are in the preset; masks or Lens Blur only if a face needs to pop. Check it on a phone, then export.

Never use the Pro Church Tools presets: they cap whites at 229, 205 or 196.
