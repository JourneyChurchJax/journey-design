1. Make series/<slug>/. Every series lives in its own folder and is free to look nothing like the last one.
  2. Write skin.html first: one face, one palette, one image idea, one device. Decide those before opening a design tool.
  3. Build the package — title.html (1920 × 1080), announce.html (1080), story, and a lower-third skin. Add sermon-title.html and scripture.html for the weekly boards.
  4. Tag every card group="Series · <Name>" so the series groups itself in the tab.
  5. Honour the contract: logo corner and size, Scripture in Newsreader. Nothing else carries over.

A free look is not free copy. No exclamation points, no invented Scripture, no invented dates. Four to six series a year.
